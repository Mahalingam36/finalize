Eight hours. No safety net.

RoboForge isn't a workshop with a demo bolted on. Bring your own components and models, and the task is handed to you on the spot — no pre-building, no head start. What you finish by the deadline is what gets judged, and the judges' decision is final. That pressure is the point: it's where teams find out what they're actually capable of.

300+ Participants expected
75+ Teams
8 Hours, unbroken

One theme: Robotics

No sub-tracks to choose between. Every team builds against the same robotics theme — the specific task is revealed on the spot.

Theme

Robotics

Bring your own components and models. The problem statement is handed out at the venue — build, wire, and code your way to a working solution before the clock runs out.

Rules

Simple, and non-negotiable.

  • Rule

    Bring your own components & models

    Participants must arrive with their own robotics components and models — none will be supplied at the venue.

  • Rule

    Task given on the spot

    The problem statement is revealed only after reporting — no advance building, no pre-written code for the task itself.

  • Rule

    Judges' decision is final

    All scoring and placement decisions made by the judging panel are final and binding.

How the 8 hours run

One day, one clock, same for every team.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Reporting & inauguration

    Check in, get your components inspected, and hear the ground rules.

  2. 9:30 AM

    Task revealed on the spot

    The clock starts here. Teams see the task for the first time and start building.

  3. 1:00 PM

    Mentor checkpoint

    Mentors do a floor walk. Flag blockers now, not at hour 7.

  4. 5:00 PM

    Submissions close

    Build freeze. What's on the table is what's judged.

  5. 5:30 PM

    Judging & demos

    Five minutes in front of the panel to make the case for what you built.

  6. 6:30 PM

    Results & closing

    Winners announced, prizes handed out, everyone goes home.
